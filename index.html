About The Event

Avinashi Vetri Marathon 2026 is a flagship social awareness initiative organized by Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) - Avinashi North-West Union, in association with Pasumai Ingarangal.

Our core mission is to build a Drug-Free Tamil Nadu and a Greener Tamil Nadu. By bringing together students, youth, and fitness enthusiasts from all walks of life, this marathon aims to inspire healthy living, eradicate substance abuse, and promote environmental conservation for a brighter tomorrow.
