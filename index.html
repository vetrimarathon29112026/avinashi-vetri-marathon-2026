About The Event

Avinashi Vetri Marathon is a social awareness initiative organized under the banner of Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), with the vision of creating a healthier, safer, and greener Tamil Nadu.

This initiative aims to bring together people from all walks of life, especially the younger generation, to promote a drug-free society, encourage sports and physical fitness, and create awareness about environmental protection. Through the spirit of running, we strive to inspire positive lifestyle changes, build social responsibility, and motivate every individual to contribute towards a better future.
